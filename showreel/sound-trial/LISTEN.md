# Listen for these

Play `showreel.mp4` (synth) and `showreel-sfx-trial.mp4` (recorded CC0 sounds) side by side. The picture is
bit-identical (video MD5 `45382a4b…`) and both play at −14.1 LUFS, so only the sound changes. The checks can't
answer the questions below; your ears can.
