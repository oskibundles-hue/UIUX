**Levels of Skills (Definitions)**
*   **Level 1 Skill:** Deterministic and simple. Usually requires only a URL or single input without extra expertise needed at the time of invocation [33:14].
*   **Level 2 Skill:** Requires "real expertise baked in." Involves a feedback loop where the user inputs their own knowledge during the build [44:54].

**The Four Steps to Build a Level 2 Skill** [46:53]
1.  **Ground it:** Brain dump everything you know. Hand over frameworks and research.
2.  **Let it work:** Give it raw material and let it produce. Do not correct it yet.
3.  **Give it feedback:** Correct it. Say why, not just what. Two or three rounds.
4.  **Capture it:** Tell it to keep what just happened. Your corrections are now rules.

**House Rules for Blog Writing (SOP)** [1:00:08]
*   Zero em dashes.
*   Title Case H2s.
*   Level 1/2/3 numerals.
*   On-the-nose titles.
*   FAQ as standard, and FAQ content without FAQPage schema.
