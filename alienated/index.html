Origin

Dead air,
worn loud.

ALIENATED started with a feeling everyone knows and nobody admits: being out of reach on purpose. The missed call. The message left on read. The voicemail that fills up and never gets heard.

We made it a wardrobe. Sorry I was high. Leave a message. Not an apology — a uniform. Hardware heavy enough to feel, blackletter loud enough to read across a room, cut close and cropped so it moves the way you do.

This isn’t fast fashion dressed in black. It’s a small, deliberate world for people who’d rather disappear than perform.

How we make it

Made small.

Returns & Exchanges

Unworn pieces can be returned or exchanged within 14 days of delivery, with tags and the numbered woven tag still attached.

  • Email us to start a return — we’ll send instructions.
  • Size exchanges ship free (it runs small — most people size up).
  • Refunds go to your original payment method once we receive the piece.
  • Any final-sale items are marked at checkout.

Care

The hardware is real metal and the graphics are appliqué — treat each piece like the collectible it is.

  • Turn inside out. Machine wash cold on gentle, or hand wash.
  • Do not bleach. Wash darks separately the first few washes.
  • Hang or lay flat to dry — never tumble dry; heat loosens studs and stresses the appliqué.
  • Cool iron only, inside out, avoiding studs and print.
  • Store on a sturdy hanger; the studded pieces carry weight.

Sizing & Fit

Everything runs cropped, low-rise and close to the body, and it runs small by design — if you’re between sizes, size up.

FAQ

Do you restock?
No. Each collection is a single numbered run of 200 per piece. When it’s gone, it’s gone — no restocks, no second pressings. The next drop is a new transmission entirely.
Are the studs real metal?
Yes — antique-silver pyramid studs, applied by hand. They have real weight and won’t peel or crack like plastic.
How does it fit?
Fitted, cropped and low-rise. It runs small on purpose — most people size up, especially for the skirt. The size guide has full body and garment measurements.
How do I track my order?
You’ll get a tracking link by email when your order ships. Can’t find it? Leave us a message with your order number and we’ll resend it.
What does “numbered edition” mean?
Every piece is one of 200, with its number on a woven tag. Your number is assigned when your order is packed.
Where do you ship?
Worldwide, fully tracked, free over $250. International duties are the buyer’s responsibility.
