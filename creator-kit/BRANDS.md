# The two brands

Omarie works for two partnered companies. Which one a video belongs to decides
its overlays, its language, and often its hook.

## Formula Dynamics — the shop

The build and service side: wheels, exhaust, suspension, tuning, body kits,
PPF, ceramic coating, paint correction, detailing.

- **Overlays:** the FD vertical starter pack — logo bugs, service lower thirds,
  title cards, CTAs, partner cards (IPE, NV Forged, RYFT).
- **Content that belongs here:** anything in the shop. Installs, bay work,
  detailing, build progress, the fit-out itself.
- **CTAs that fit:** book your build, get a quote, find your fitment,
  we service what we build.

## Supercar Experience — the rental side

The supercar rental business. The "ANTI STOCK SUPERCAR EXPERIENCE" shirt is
Supercar Experience merch — **it is not a brand marker for the footage.**
Omarie wears it while working in the FD shop, so the shirt tells you nothing
about which company the video is for.

- **Overlays:** none exist yet. This is a real gap — see below.
- **Content that belongs here:** picking up and dropping off vehicles,
  customer handovers, the rental fleet, experience days.
- **CTAs that fit:** booking a car, availability, the experience itself.

## How to tell them apart

Judge by **what is happening**, not by what he is wearing.

| Signal | Brand |
|---|---|
| Work being done to a car in a bay | Formula Dynamics |
| Shop build-out, tools, installs | Formula Dynamics |
| Collecting or delivering a vehicle | Supercar Experience |
| Customer handover, keys, driving out | Supercar Experience |
| Anti Stock shirt | tells you nothing |

trim, ceiling panels, counter build, epoxy, wheel and brake work, cars on
lifts. The FD overlay pack applies to all of it.
