# SFX kit v2: licence

Every sound in this folder was synthesised in this repo by
`creator-kit/experiments/edit-refs/gen_sfx_kit.py`: numpy oscillators, filtered
noise and envelopes, with fixed random seeds, so a re-run writes the same files.
No samples, recordings, presets or downloads went into them.

They are original work, free to use in any video (Anti Stock, client work or
anything else), with no attribution needed. The palette was inspired by the
@jerrzyo SFX style and the @cinamiti rapid zoom; nothing was copied from either.

Regenerate: `python3 creator-kit/experiments/edit-refs/gen_sfx_kit.py`
