## Edited: 23-44-19 -> detailing Reel

Cut first because it is the only detailing material in the session and visually
distinct from the build-out footage.

Silence-cut at -22 dB / 0.25s: **91.1s -> 69.0s**, 26 segments, 2.7s average.
Delivered as 2160x3840 / 29.97fps / 10.0 Mbps / 84.3 MB.

### Two limits hit on this export

**Bitrate floor vs target size.** 75 MB across 69s works out to 8.7 Mbps, below
the 10 Mbps floor for 4K. autocut held the floor and came in at 84.3 MB rather
than starve the picture. To actually land 75 MB, cut to ~60s.

**Loudness lands at -15.3 LUFS, not -14.** Single-pass `loudnorm` measures and
corrects in one go, so it approximates. Within ~1.5 LU is fine — Instagram
normalises anyway — but two-pass would hit it exactly.

### Silence thresholds are per-clip

-26 dB suited the walkthrough footage but removed only 10% here and left 6.9s
shots. -22 dB gives 69s at 2.7s. Sweep with `--dry-run` on each new clip rather
than assuming a setting carries over.

### Exposure varies clip to clip

Graded, 23-44-19 lands at YAVG ~75 against the reference's 46. That is correct,
not a fault: the LUT is a fixed curve, this scene is genuinely brighter (work
lights under a car), and forcing it to 46 would crush the underbody detail.

Session YAVG ranges 90–122, so a single shared LUT will leave the bright clips
bright. If every post should match, that wants a per-clip `match_grade.py` pass
against the reference instead of one LUT.
