These are YOURS. Not Formula Dynamics, not Supercar Experience. The accent is
#FBD101 - the gold from your own burned-in captions, which is the one colour
already unmistakably yours across every post. A handle bug wearing a client's
brand colours reads as an ad for the client.

WHAT IS IN HERE
---------------
handle_9x16_<platform>_<position>_<colour>.png   12 files
    platform  instagram | youtube | tiktok
    position  bottom-left (the streamer convention) | top-left
    colour    white for dark footage | black for bright footage

handle_9x16_all-stacked_<colour>.png              2 files
    All three, stacked, with a FOLLOW label. For an end card or the last few
    seconds - three rows of text is a lot to leave sitting on the frame.

handle_9x16_all-bar_<colour>.png                  2 files
    All three on one line, centred. Reads in about a second, which is what you
    want if it is only up for the last three.

WHICH ONE
---------
Posting to Instagram   -> use the instagram single. Showing someone your other
                          handles on the platform they are already on is wasted
                          space; the ask is to follow you SOMEWHERE ELSE.
Posting to TikTok/YT   -> use the instagram single, same logic in reverse.
Cross-posting one cut  -> the bar, last 3 seconds only.
End card              -> the stacked panel.

Use top-left when the interesting thing is in the lower half of the frame,
which - filming a car on a lift - is most of the time.

BURNING THEM IN
---------------
  ./overlay.sh reel.mp4 out.mp4 \
    "overlays-handles/handle_9x16_instagram_bottom-left_white.png:0"

Omit the END time so it stays up for the whole video. That is the point of a
handle bug: it survives a repost or a screen-record, which is exactly when
someone cannot tap through to your profile.

GEOMETRY
--------
All singles are 88px tall, the same height as the Formula Dynamics and Supercar
Experience logo bugs, so they sit on the same optical line and can share a
frame without looking stacked by accident.

Do not run a handle bug bottom-left at the same time as a lower third or a CTA
panel - they occupy the same band. Move the handle to top-left.

Rebuild:
  python3 creator-kit/scripts/build_handle_overlays.py \
      --out overlays-handles --verify
