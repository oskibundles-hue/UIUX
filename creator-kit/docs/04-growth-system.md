# The growth system

A page grows when it does the same recognisable thing repeatedly, well enough
that the algorithm can learn who to show it to. Random good posts do not
compound. Formats do.

## Content pillars

Five lanes. Every post belongs to one. This stops you making the same video
forty times and gives Instagram a coherent signal about who your audience is.

| Pillar | Job | Roughly |
|---|---|---|
| **World / immersion** | Reach. The visually stunning stuff nobody has seen. | 30% |
| **Explainer** | Reach beyond the niche. For people who don't know what LARP is. | 25% |
| **Craft / kit** | Saves and authority. Costume, armour, weapons, builds. | 20% |
| **Human / community** | Follows. Why people come back — you and the people around you. | 15% |
| **Lifestyle vlog** | Conversion. Turns a viewer of *the content* into a follower of *you*. | 10% |

The explainer pillar is the one most people in a visual niche underuse, and
it's where your largest reach lives. There are far more people curious about
this world than people already in it.

## Repeatable series

A series beats a one-off because returning viewers already know the format, and
the algorithm has an easier job. Pick three and run them weekly.

- **"Things nobody tells you about…"** — first events, camping, rain, kit, cost
- **Kit breakdown** — one piece of gear per post, what it cost, what it's for
- **Getting ready** — transformation from you to character, reveal first
- **Day two** — the honest, tired, funny reality of a multi-day event
- **Explaining this to my non-LARP friends** — react/explain format
- **Rating my own kit honestly** — self-deprecating, high comment rate

## Cadence

- **4–5 Reels a week.** Below 3 you lose momentum; above 7 they cannibalise
  each other's audition window.
- **Never two Reels within a few hours.** They compete for the same test pool.
- **Stories daily-ish.** Stories don't drive reach, they drive retention of the
  followers you already have. Different job, still worth doing.
- **1–2 carousels a week.** Carousels get a second bite: Instagram re-serves
  them to people who didn't engage first time.

## The first 90 minutes

Reach is decided by an audition — a small test pool whose behaviour determines
whether you get expanded distribution. So:

- Post when your audience is actually online. Your own Insights beat any
  generic "best time to post" chart.
- **Be present for the first hour.** Reply to every comment, properly, with more
  than one word. Reply-to-comment activity is itself engagement.
- Reply to DMs. A DM conversation is a strong relationship signal.
- Do not delete a post that underperforms. It does not help, and you lose the
  data.

## Weekly diagnostic

Track these five in Insights. Each one has a specific fix.

| Metric | If it's low | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **3-second retention** | People swipe immediately | Your hook. First frame, first line, on-screen text. |
| **Average watch time** | They leave mid-video | Pacing. Cut harder, shorten shots, add pattern interrupts. |
| **Sends per reach** | Nobody shares it | Your CTA, and whether the video is *about* somebody. Add the "send this to…" line. |
| **Follows per reach** | Reach without growth | They liked the video, not you. Add more of you — face, voice, opinion. |
| **Saves** | Not useful enough | Add practical value: costs, how-to, gear specifics. |

Working targets to aim at, not laws: 3-second retention above ~60%, average
watch time above half the video's length, sends above ~1% of reach. Treat your
own best-performing post as the benchmark and try to beat it.

## 30-day plan

**Week 1 — baseline and system**
Set up capture settings and the LUT workflow. Post 4 Reels, one from each of
the top four pillars. Do not chase reach yet; you are collecting data. Write
down the numbers from the diagnostic table for each post.

**Week 2 — format testing**
Post 5 Reels across three different series formats. Same grade, same caption
structure, different formats. You are looking for which *format* outperforms,
not which topic.

**Week 3 — double down**
Take the two best-performing formats from week 2 and make three more of each.
Vary the subject, keep the structure identical. This is where a format becomes
a series.

**Week 4 — scale what works**
Post 5 Reels, at least 3 in your proven format. Take your single best post and
remake it with a different hook — reposting a proven concept with a new opening
is not lazy, it is how every large account works.

At the end of the month you should have a format you can execute without
thinking about it. That is the actual deliverable.

## Cross-posting

Export once, post to Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Same 1080×1920 file
works on all three. Remove the burned-in TikTok watermark issue by exporting
from CapCut rather than downloading from TikTok — a visible competitor
watermark suppresses reach on Instagram.

## What not to bother with

- **Buying followers or engagement.** It poisons your audience signal, so
  Instagram shows your posts to people who don't care, and reach falls.
- **Follow/unfollow.** Same problem, plus it gets accounts actioned.
- **Engagement pods.** The signal is from accounts unlike your target audience,
  which actively misdirects your distribution.
- **Posting 30 hashtags.** No measurable benefit.
- **Deleting underperformers.** Does nothing for future reach.

The only durable lever is: make something people watch to the end and send to a
friend. Everything in this kit is in service of that.
