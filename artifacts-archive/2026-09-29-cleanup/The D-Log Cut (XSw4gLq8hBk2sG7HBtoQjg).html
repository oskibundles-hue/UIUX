Where the D-Log stops

Editing stopped at the end of 2026-09-05. Nothing from 09-06 onward has been touched, so your test video on the normal-colour footage is still the first thing that happens to it.

The line sits between DJI_20260905151449_0017_D (last D-Log) and DJI_20260906114140_0020_D (first normal). Three things change together there: the black floor drops from about 39 to about 5, saturation roughly doubles, and the camera moves from 3840×3840 open-gate to 3840×2880 and then 3840×2160. The _D in the filename is not a reliable signal — every raw carries it, including the normal-colour ones.

Nothing here repeats the published series

The Vlog Cut and Fast Cut pages were built from clips 01–18, which match the D-Log raws by duration: 09-02 0011–0018, 0020, 0021, 0023 and 09-03 0024–0030. All of those were left alone. Every source clip listed above sits outside that set — including 09-03 0031–0040, which the published series never reached.

Two things worth knowing

The black car is an Aston Martin Vantage. The badge and the RBW3V12 plate confirm it. That answers the open question about MR6’s lower third reading “Black car on the lift”.

No spoken captions. There is no transcription available on this machine, so these carry hook lines, titles and chapter bars — all written — but no dialogue captions. Titles are set in Impact rather than Anton/Archivo, which are not installed locally.

Grade

Rebuilt against 03 Grade Reference, which is HLG BT.2020 and had to be tone-mapped to Rec.709 first. Matching is done on luminance only — per-channel matching was pulling a cool cast onto clips that should be warm — then grey balance is set explicitly to +2.0 R−B, with black lift 0.03 applied last so it survives the contrast curve.
