What's new under the hood

How set 2 gets depth

Cut-outs from Apple's own Vision

The cars and Omarie are cut out with the Mac's built-in Vision framework: a person matte on every frame for B4, a car matte for each freeze. No model download. Where Vision merged a parked car into the Urus's roof, a hand line along the hood fixes it.

lib/matte.swift · config2.json exclude

Three layers, not one

Each frame can have graphics in front, graphics behind the subject (the subject is laid back on top through its matte) and a window layer that shows the next shot. Every layer gets the same real motion blur.

kit2.html passes · lib/compose2.py

A lock that holds a surface

C5 tracks the Urus's side as a plane: a perspective transform on every camera frame (86 or more matching points each). The brackets, grid and ruler are drawn through it, so they turn as the camera passes.

lib/plane_track.py · lib/data/plane.json

Real slow motion, real sound

The camera shoots 59.94 fps, so C5's half speed uses every source frame with nothing blended. I6's tape stop is the clip's own sound slowing to a stop; no sound is added anywhere in the reel.
