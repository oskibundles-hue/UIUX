# Plan: bring local customers to Formula Dynamics

Written 24 Nov 2026 for Omarie. Built on the referral kit in this folder.
Goal: send Las Vegas owners of exotic and performance cars to Formula Dynamics (FD),
so they book service or buy parts with the code OMARIE, and the commission is tracked.

The numbers below are starting targets, not measured results. After four weeks, the
tracker shows which places work. Then do more of those and drop the rest.

---

## Step 0. Lock it in with Nate (this week, before any cards go out)

1. Send Nate the drafted text (`TEXT-TO-NATE.md`). You send it yourself.
2. Wait for his five answers: % on service, % on parts, before or after tax, any offer,
   and who notes the code on the work order.
3. Answer 4 matters most. If nobody writes the code down, a referral cannot be proven.
4. Put his numbers on the tracker's SETTINGS tab. If he sets an offer or a different code,
   the cards get rebuilt (one line each in `build_referral_kit.py`).
5. Print about 100 business cards from `referral-card_print_3.5x2.png`. The print cost is
   yours to decide. Any print shop takes a 3.5 x 2 in card with bleed.

While you wait: write your warm list (Step 1, place A). It costs nothing and needs no answer.

## Step 2. The weekly routine (about 3 hours a week)

| When | What | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday, 20 min** | Open the tracker. Send script 5 to anyone who said "maybe" 7 or more days ago. Plan this weekend's meet. | All follow-ups sent |
| **Tue to Thu, 15 min a day** | Warm texts (script 1) from your list. One problem reply (script 2) when you see one. | 5 warm texts a week |
| **After any shoot** | Send each owner you filmed one clip of their car, then script 3 with the phone card. | Every owner you filmed |
| **Weekend, 1 to 2 hours** | One meet or Cars & Coffee. Talk first, card second. Get their Instagram. | 10 cards handed out, 3 handles |
| **Sunday, 10 min** | DM the handles from the meet (script 3 + animated card). Log any booking. | 3 DMs |

That is about 20 people a week who hear about FD from you.

### Rules at a meet

- Ask the organiser if it is fine to hand out cards. Some meets don't allow it.
- Never put cards on windshields. Give them to the owner by hand.
- Talk about the car first. Hand the card only when service, a problem or a mod comes up.
- One card each. No crowd pitch.

## Step 4. How each referral goes into the tracker

Open `FD-referral-tracker.xlsx`, tab **LOG**. One row per car that comes in.

1. **The day they say they booked:** add a row. Fill in Date in, Customer (first name and
   last initial), Car, and **How they came in**. Use one of these words every time:
   `warm text`, `filmed`, `meet`, `instagram`, `problem`. That word shows which place works.
2. **When the work is done:** ask the shop for the invoice number and total. Fill in Work
   done, Service or Parts, Invoice # and Invoice total. The commission fills itself in.
3. **Count it as owed only when** the shop confirms your code is on that invoice.
4. **When Nate pays:** put "Yes" in Paid to Omarie and the date. The SUMMARY tab shows what
   is still owed.

People who said "maybe" don't go in the tracker. Keep them in one phone note with the
date, so the Monday follow-up finds them.

## Step 5. What a good month looks like

These are starting guesses. The tracker replaces them after the first month.

| | Week 1 | Month 1 | Month 2 on |
|---|---|---|---|
| People reached | 20 | 80 | 80 a month |
| Cards handed out | 10 | 40 | 40 a month |
| "Maybe" replies | 2 | 8 | 8+ |
| Cars booked | 0 to 1 | 1 to 2 | 2 to 4 a month |

Warm contacts and filmed owners usually book first. Meets and cold DMs take longer.

**Monthly check (first Monday of the month, 15 min):** count bookings by "How they came in".
Spend next month's time on the top two. Send Nate the month's list, so you both agree on what
is owed.

## Never do these

- Don't promise prices, horsepower, results or turnaround times. Nate quotes those.
- Don't say "discount". If Nate sets an offer, use his exact words.
- Don't post as FD or on FD's accounts. This is you, telling people you trust the shop.
- Don't send the same person more than two messages.
