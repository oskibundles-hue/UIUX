# Templates & Reference

## `safe-zone-guides/`

Drop the guide matching your canvas onto the **top** track while editing.

- **Red bands** — covered by platform UI. No logos, no text, no faces.
- **White lines** — thirds grid, for composing shots.
- **Green cross** — exact frame centre.

**Hide or delete this layer before exporting.**

The 9:16 zones are sized for TikTok/Reels/Shorts: status bar at the top,
caption and CTA at the bottom, like/comment/share rail on the right. The other
ratios use a conservative 5% margin.

## `reference-posters/`

The four campaign posters — Rolls-Royce, McLaren, Maserati, Ferrari.

These are the art-direction reference for the whole video system. Note what
they consistently do:

- Logo top-centre, generous clear space
- Marque name in white/black, **UPGRADES** in red italic beneath
- Accent stripe splitting the two lines
- Service list as a single row, dot-separated or in chips
- Car occupies the bottom two-thirds, motion streaks behind it
- Background is either near-black or near-white — never mid-grey, never busy

The title cards in `../03-overlays/title-cards/` follow the same two-line
structure, so a video opens looking like the poster it belongs with.
