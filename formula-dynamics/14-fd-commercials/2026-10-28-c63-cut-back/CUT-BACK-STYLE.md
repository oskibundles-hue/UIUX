# Cut Back (style, picked by Omarie 12 Oct 2026)

A short Reel style taken from our YouTube research (the vlog-lifestyle playbook's study of HiddenQuan, Brez Scales,
Casey Neistat and five other channels, plus the hooks doc). First used for the FD C63 exhaust post, 28 Oct.

- **One car, one question.** Ask it on screen from frame 1 ("WHAT DID WE DO TO THIS C63?") over the payoff shot,
  the flash-forward the research rates as the strongest open. Then cut back: before, the work, payoff.
- **Graphics are seasoning.** The question, one or two typed word pops ("NEW."), small date stamps ("SEP 29", "OCT 7").
  No brackets, callouts, banners, bugs, figures or progress rails. The brand mark shows only on a 1 to 1.5 s end card.
- **Brand lightly.** The workstream's display face (FD: Bebas Neue), white type, the brand colour once as an underline
  under the subject word, and the official mark and stripe at the end only.
- **The car's own sound carries it.** Bare revs open and close the piece, with no music unless it's licensed.
- **Length and pace.** 15 to 18 s and 10 to 15 picture changes a minute, quicker through the parts and held long on the payoff.
- **Rules.** Real footage of one car only. Redact plates and any meter readouts. No price, fitment or performance claims.
