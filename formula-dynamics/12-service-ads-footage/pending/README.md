## Service Pricing — the à la carte counterpart

Three prices, one per panel, 1.8 s each. Deliberately on the **same car as the
annual package**: together they read as one menu — pay per job, or buy the year
for $3,999. Conditions carry over unchanged in the ticker: **oil included, pads
not included.**

The price is the hero. Kicker names the job, the figure is set large — the same
shape as the frosted panels on the dealer templates, where the label is small
and the number carries.

## A trap worth remembering

`--partner ryft` is **silently dropped when `--title-block` is set** — the title
block replaces the lower third, and the partner plate is a lower third. No error,
the layer just is not in the cue sheet. Same class of failure as passing the CTA
group along with the CTA slug. Read the dry run.

Here the panels name RYFT directly, which is stronger anyway: it says the part
is on *this* car rather than that the shop knows the brand.
