# Partner Logos

**Empty on purpose.** Partner logos are their intellectual property, so no
artwork is generated for them here.

Drop official files in this folder as you receive them:

```
nv-forged/
ipe/
ryft/
```

## Getting official assets

Most brands publish a media or press kit — check their site footer for
"Press", "Media", or "Brand Assets", or email their marketing contact. Ask for
**vector (SVG/EPS) or transparent PNG at 2000 px+**.

Never screenshot a logo off a website. It looks low-resolution next to your own
vector mark, and it usually breaches their brand guidelines.

## Before co-locking with the FD logo

Read each partner's brand guidelines. Common requirements:

- Minimum clear space around their mark
- Minimum reproduction size
- Approved colour variants only (many prohibit recolouring)
- Some prohibit placing their logo adjacent to another brand's entirely

When in doubt, put the partner logo on its own frame rather than locked to
yours — and use the ready-made `OFFICIAL PARTNER` lower thirds in
`../lower-thirds/`.
