# Shot Lists & Video Formulas

Seven repeatable formats. Pick one, shoot the list, edit to the beat sheet.
Repeatable structure is what makes a channel look professional — and it makes
editing fast.

Every formula uses the same skeleton:

```
0–3 s    HOOK      Best frame you own. Earn the scroll-stop.
3–8 s    CONTEXT   What car, what's happening.
8–20 s   PAYOFF    The thing they came for.
20–27 s  PROOF     Detail, quality, craft.
27–30 s  CTA       End card.
```

---

## 1. The Reveal — body kits

**Hook:** the finished car, moving, low angle. Never open on a "before".

| Shot | Notes |
|---|---|
| Low front 3/4, car rolling in | Camera at bumper height |
| Slow pan along the new aero | Handheld, gentle |
| Detail: splitter, diffuser, canards | Macro, shallow depth |
| Carbon weave close-up | Rake light across the weave |
| Wide static, full car | Hold 3 s — the "hero" frame |
| Before shot | **Last**, as contrast, not first |

**Overlays:** `title_9x16_body-kits_dark` → `lt_9x16_service_body-kits` →
`badge_body-kits_dark` → `endcard_9x16_dark`

---

## 2. Sound Check — exhaust

**Hook:** the sound, in the first half second. Cut to the loudest frame first.

| Shot | Notes |
|---|---|
| Tailpipe, engine off, tight | Establish the hardware |
| Cold start, tips in frame | **Record clean audio** — this is the whole video |
| Rev, exterior | Mic away from the car; phone mics clip |
| Rev, interior — driver POV | |
| Valve open/close comparison | Split screen or hard cut |
| Drive-by, static camera | The money shot |

**Audio is the product here.** Don't bury it under music — drop the music bed
under the revs, or mute it entirely. If the exhaust note clips, the video fails.

**Overlays:** `title_9x16_sound-check_dark` → `lt_9x16_service_exhaust` →
`lt_9x16_partner_ipe` (if applicable) → `endcard_9x16_dark`

## 3. Fitment — wheels

**Hook:** the wheel gap closing, or the first wheel going on.

| Shot | Notes |
|---|---|
| Old wheel, static | Brief |
| Wheel off, hub bare | |
| New wheel lifted into frame | Slow motion, 60 fps |
| Bolting up, torque wrench | Signals competence |
| Car dropping off the lift | Slow motion |
| Rolling shot, wheels turning | Track alongside |
| Static 3/4, full car | Hero |

**Overlays:** `title_9x16_wheels_dark` → `lt_9x16_service_wheels` →
`lt_9x16_partner_nv-forged` → `endcard_9x16_dark`

---

## 4. Dyno / Tune — tuning

**Hook:** the number. Put the peak figure on screen in the first 2 s.

| Shot | Notes |
|---|---|
| Car strapped to the dyno | Establish |
| Laptop, live graph | Over-shoulder |
| Wheels spinning under load | Slow motion |
| Operator watching, focused | Human element |
| Screen with the final figure | Hold — this is the payoff |
| Owner's reaction | If you can get it, use it |

Put before/after figures on screen in Bebas Neue, gain figure in red.

**Overlays:** `title_9x16_dyno-results_dark` → `lt_9x16_service_tuning` →
`badge_tuning_dark` → `endcard_9x16_dark`

---

## 5. Before & After

**Hook:** the after. Always lead with the after.

Structure: after (2 s) → hard cut to before (2 s) → build montage (10 s) →
after again, held longer (5 s) → end card.

Lock the camera to the **same position** for both states. A tripod mark on the
floor makes the cut land. Same angle, same light, same lens.

**Overlays:** `title_9x16_before-after_dark` → `endcard_9x16_dark`

---

## 6. Install Day — timelapse

**Hook:** the last 2 s of the timelapse, played first.

Lock a phone on a tripod for the whole job. Shoot 1 frame/sec, or record long
takes and speed to 10–20×. Cut in real-time close-ups of the interesting
moments so it isn't only timelapse.

**Overlays:** `title_9x16_install-day_dark` → service lower third →
`endcard_9x16_dark`

---

## 7. Service & Maintenance — the trust builder

Less glamorous, converts well. Shows you're a real shop, not just a parts
fitter.

| Shot | Notes |
|---|---|
| Car on the lift, clean bay | A tidy shop is the message |
| Fluid change, close and clean | |
| Torque wrench, gloves on | Competence signals |
| Diagnostic screen | |
| Finished car, wiped down | |

**Overlays:** `lt_9x16_service_service` → `endcard_9x16_dark`

Post these between the loud upgrade videos. They're what makes an owner trust
you with a car worth six figures.

## Shooting basics

- **Film 60 fps.** Slow motion later without stuttering.
- **Golden hour or full shade.** Midday sun blows out paint and clips highlights.
- **Move the camera or move the car.** Never both static.
- **Clean the car first.** Every time. Dust reads as neglect at 4K.
- **Kill reflections** — you, your phone, the bay lights. Watch the panels.
- **Wipe the lens** between setups.
- **Record audio separately** for anything exhaust-related.
