# Exhaust partners — what we can say on screen

Everything here is taken from the manufacturers' own sites, fetched 9 September
2026. Anything not on this page has not been verified and should not go on
screen as a claim.

---

## RYFT — `ryft.co`

**What they make.** Titanium exhaust systems, in their own words *"crafted from
100% Titanium and are carefully handmade by our team, delivering an unmistakable
and distinctive acoustic signature."*

**Platforms they cover**

| Marque | Models listed |
|---|---|
| Ferrari | 296 GTB, 458, 488, 812, F8 Tributo, F12 Berlinetta, LaFerrari, Monza SP1/SP2, SF90 Stradale |
| Lamborghini | Aventador, Huracán, Revuelto, Urus |
| Porsche | 991 GT2 RS, 991 GT3/RS, 992 Carrera, Turbo, GT3 variants |
| McLaren | 720S, 750S, 765LT, Artura, Senna |
| Audi | R8 V10 |

**Beyond exhaust.** Carbon fibre aero packages (modular), forged wheels with
bespoke finishes, and height-adjustable performance suspension through a
partnership with Moton — the suspension side of the line Formula Dynamics
carries.

**Named example.** The Lamborghini Revuelto HYPR system, which they describe as
*"a symphony of performance and sound."*

**Safe on-screen lines**
- 100% titanium
- Handmade
- Distinctive acoustic signature

**Fitment confirmed:** the shop's **Ferrari Roma carries a RYFT exhaust.** That is what lets the Roma free-tune ad name RYFT on screen — a partner
name over a car is a claim about that car, and this one is now backed.

## iPE — Innotech Performance Exhaust — `ipeofficial.com`

**Who they are.** Founded 2010 by Gary Chien, built out of Jim Header
Industrial (1998) and Jim Technologies (2002).

**Materials.** T304 stainless steel and Grade 1 titanium, which they describe as
*"high-grade aerospace"* materials. TIG welded — a technique they note is
*"suitable for joining very thin materials"* with better precision and
durability.

**The valvetronic system — this is the part worth a graphic.** Two ways to
control it: a remote control module, or an OBDII gesture sensor. Three modes:

| Mode | What it does, in their words |
|---|---|
| **ON** | *"Valves are fully open for maximum airflow allowing for better performance and increased sound volume"* |
| **OFF** | *"Valves are closed, reducing exhaust volume. Allowing a comfortable drive"* |
| **AUTO** | *"The system detects the fuel pedal signal and adjusts the valve movement accordingly and automatically for a dynamic drive"* |

That three-state behaviour is the strongest visual idea either partner gives
us — the R1 exhaust reel already animates a closed-to-open valve moment, and
this is the same argument with a manufacturer's name on it.

**Platforms named on the site.** Porsche 992 GT3/GT3 RS, 991 GT3/GT3 RS,
Ferrari Roma, Lamborghini Aventador SVJ. Full systems and catback.

**Also.** Forged magnesium wheels — MFP-07, marketed as *"F1 Lightweight
Technology"*, and MFL-03, *"35% lighter than OEM"*.

**Safe on-screen lines**
- Valvetronic
- T304 stainless / Grade 1 titanium
- Remote or OBDII gesture control
- ON / OFF / AUTO

## Larini

Formula Dynamics is the **North American distributor**. That is a distribution
relationship, not a claim about the product, and it is already the backbone of
the R1 exhaust reel and the R5 authority reel. No URL is published here because
none has been verified — get the official one from Larini before putting it on
screen.

## Opus — `opusinnovations.com/exhaust`

**Materials.** *"Inconel, titanium, aerospace-grade stainless."* Headers are
hand-formed; runners are heat-shielded.

**The engineering claim worth using.** *"Equal-length headers derived from
motorsport research"* — the point being synchronised exhaust pulses across the
cylinders. High-flow catalytic converters *"wrapped in thermal mesh"* hold
temperature.

**Sound.** Every muffler chamber is *"acoustically mapped and tuned to harmonic
perfection."* They call the result *"raw, refined, and utterly intoxicating."*

**Build quality.** Built to order — *"tailored to your vehicle, your vision, and
your obsession"* — with *"each weld bead inspected under magnification"* and gas
channelling they describe as *"zero turbulence."*

**Safe on-screen lines**
- Inconel · titanium · aerospace-grade stainless
- Equal-length headers
- Hand-formed, built to order

**Not on the page, so not claimable:** model names, weight savings, valve
control, platform coverage, warranty terms. Get those from Opus before any of it
goes on screen.

## A standing rule on partner marks

Partner **logos** are third-party trademarks and are deliberately not generated
in this kit. Request the official asset pack from each manufacturer before any
logo goes to air. A partner **name** set in our own type, and their own public
URL, are fine — that is what the URL graphic in `partner.js` does, and it is
why it uses type rather than a logo.
