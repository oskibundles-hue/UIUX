An exhaust valve is a flap that gives the car two exhausts in one. Closed, the gas goes the long way through a resonator section, which keeps cold starts and cruising quiet. Open, it goes straight to the tips and the car gets louder.

On vacuum-valve cars a solenoid does the switching, and from the factory it usually follows Sport mode. A controller like FD's Muffler Valve Control Kit puts a 2-button remote in your hand instead.
