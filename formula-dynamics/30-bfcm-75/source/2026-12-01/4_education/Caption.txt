Before you fit lowering springs, look at the gap between the top of your tire and the lip of the arch. Lowering the car brings the arch down toward the tire, so that gap is the room your wheel has to move.

Measure it, subtract the drop, and remember wheel and tire size change the answer. Send us the car, sizes and a side photo and we will work it out with you.
