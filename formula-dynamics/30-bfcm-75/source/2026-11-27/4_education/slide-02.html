WHICH IS WHICH  ·  DAMPING02 / 03
Same damper, two directions

Compression

also called bump
↑

The wheel moves up toward the body and the damper shortens.

Hitting a bump or a driveway lip.

Rebound

the return stroke
↓

The wheel moves back down and the damper extends.

Coming off the bump, or over a crest.
The spring holds the car up. Damping sets how hard the damper resists each of these two motions.
