Compression or rebound? Both words are about the damper, not the spring. Compression is the wheel moving up toward the body as the damper shortens. Rebound is the wheel moving back down as it extends.

So when a KW V3 listing says "independently adjustable compression and rebound", it means two separate settings, one for each direction.
