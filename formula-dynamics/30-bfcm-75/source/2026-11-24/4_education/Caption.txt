Before you add a part to the cart, read the fit line. Take the KW V3 listing for the Ghibli: it gives the years, a chassis code, with or without Skyhook, and RWD. If a code looks wrong, ask FD. An SQ4 is all-wheel drive, so it is not covered.

With Skyhook, the listing says electronic damping has to be deactivated, and the deactivation kit is included. Height is set at the front by threaded shock bodies and at the rear by a spring perch.
