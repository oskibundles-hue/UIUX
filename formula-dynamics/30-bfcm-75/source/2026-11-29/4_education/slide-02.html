THE SIGNAL PATH  ·  PEDAL TO THROTTLE02 / 03
Three steps, every press
01

Pedal

Position sensors read how far you press. There are usually two, so the system can cross-check them.

02

ECU

The engine computer turns pedal position into a throttle opening, using its own programming.

03

Throttle body

An electric motor turns the throttle plate to the angle the ECU asks for.

A sensor reports the plate's angle
back to the ECU
So how the car answers your foot is a setting, not a fixed linkage.
