F430 owners who wince at every driveway: KW's HLS 2 hydraulic lift is listed for the 2005 to 2009 F430, Spider included, and the 2007 to 2009 Scuderia. It lifts the front axle from a switch in the cabin, and the listing says it can be used up to 50 mph.

Two variants: HLS 2 on its own, for cars already on KW coilovers, at $9,166.55 (was $9,649.00), or HLS with the KW V3 coilover kit at $12,995.05 (was $13,679.00). KW coilovers are 5% off through Dec 4 at formuladynamics.com.
