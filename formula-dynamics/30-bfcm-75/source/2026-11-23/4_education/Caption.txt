Before you pick a spacer size, measure. Park on flat ground with the wheels straight, hold a straight edge from the top centre of the fender to the base of the tire, and measure the gap to the rim face or the tire in millimetres. For a flush fit, take 2 to 3mm off: a 15mm gap usually means a 12mm spacer. Then do the rear, because it often differs.
