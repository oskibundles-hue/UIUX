Most performance rotors are two parts. The hat in the middle bolts to the hub, and the ring is the surface the pads clamp. On a floating design the ring can grow with heat and center itself, and when it wears you replace the ring and keep the hat. Drilled or slotted is the next choice, and FD's own listing notes slotted rotors are preferred for track use. Browse rotors and brake kits at formuladynamics.com.
