THREE QUESTIONS  ·  BRAKE ROTORS03 / 03
Q

Why two pieces?

On a floating design the ring can grow with heat and center itself between the pads. When it wears, the ring is replaced and the hat stays.

Q

What are the slots in the edge?

Vanes. The ring is two faces with air channels between them.

Q

Drilled or slotted?

Brembo kits list both. FD's own Ghibli kit listing says slotted-only rotors are preferred for track use.
