Rubber vs braided

Rubber hose

Typical stock line
  • Can swell under hard braking, felt as a softer pedal
  • Porous, so fluid can take on moisture more easily

Stainless braided

FD braided lines
  • The braid holds the line's shape under pressure
  • It also protects the line from the outside
