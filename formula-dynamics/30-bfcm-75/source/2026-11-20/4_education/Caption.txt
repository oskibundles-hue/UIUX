The short hose behind each wheel is usually rubber from the factory. Under hard braking rubber can swell a little, and you feel that as a softer pedal. It's also porous, so the fluid can take on moisture more easily.

A braided line wraps the inner line in stainless steel, which holds its shape under pressure and protects it from the outside. FD's lines are D.O.T. approved and listed by model, from the 348 to the MC20.
