On an F8, spacers don't lower anything. They move the wheel out toward the fender lip, so that gap is the one to look at.

Front and rear take different sizes. FD's F8 listing recommends +18mm front and +22mm rear. The +15 and +18 kits come with longer wheel bolts, and +22 and up bolt to the hub and keep your Ferrari bolts.
