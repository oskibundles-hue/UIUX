# Voice & Tone

Built on the brand values: **Performance · Precision · Passion · Quality**.

## How we sound

**Confident, not loud.** The work speaks. Don't oversell it.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Titanium valvetronic. 12 lb lighter." | "INSANE SOUND!!! 🔥🔥🔥" |
| "Fitted, aligned, torqued to spec." | "We do the best work in the game" |
| "Before: 612. After: 703." | "Massive gains!!" |
| Name the part, the car, the spec | Vague hype with no substance |

**Specific beats loud.** A part number, a weight saving, a torque figure — real
numbers are what an owner of a six-figure car responds to. Anyone can shout.

**Short sentences. Hard stops.** Match the edit.

**We're technicians who love cars,** not a hype account.

## Emoji

Sparingly. One, at most, at the end of a caption. Never in a headline, never in
an on-screen graphic, never in the middle of a sentence.

## Words we use

precision · engineered · calibrated · fitted · spec · bespoke · track-bred ·
motorsport · forged · titanium · exacting · proven

## Words to avoid

Anything unverifiable ("best in the world"), anything cheap-sounding ("deal",
"cheap", "discount"), and anything that promises a result you can't guarantee.

## On claims

Only publish performance figures you actually measured. "Up to" and "gains vary
by fuel and conditions" are honest and cost nothing. Getting caught inflating a
dyno number destroys trust with exactly the customer you want.

## On partners

Name them properly and consistently: NV Forged, IPE, RIFT, Luring. Tag their
accounts. Never imply an endorsement or a partnership tier that isn't real.
