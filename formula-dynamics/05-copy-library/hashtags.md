# Hashtags

## How to use

- **Instagram** — 8–15 tags. Mix broad reach and specific intent.
- **TikTok** — 3–5 tags. The algorithm reads your caption and audio more than
  your tags.
- **YouTube Shorts** — 2–3 in the description.

Always include the brand tags. Always add the **car make and model** — that's
the tag an actual buyer searches.

## Partner tagging

Tag partner **accounts** in the post and in the video itself — that's what
earns a reshare. Confirm each handle on their profile before publishing;
hashtags are harmless if wrong, but an @ mention that points at the wrong
account is a bad look.
