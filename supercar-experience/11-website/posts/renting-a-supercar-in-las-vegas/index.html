Renting a supercar in Las Vegas: what happens from booking to keys

Most rental pages stop at the price. Behind the Wheel films the rest: the pickups, the checks and the hand-overs. Here's how a Supercar Experience rental in Las Vegas works, from the first text to the moment the keys are yours.

Supercar Experience · Las Vegas · rates checked 6 Oct 2026

01Check you can drive it

Supercar Experience's terms ask for three things:

  • You're 25 or older. Drivers aged 21 to 24 can still rent, with a $299 underage fee (our under-25 guide).
  • A valid driver's license.
  • Valid insurance. You send the details when you book, so it's checked before you arrive.

Visiting from outside the US? Supercar Experience sells international insurance.

02Pick the car and how long

Most cars rent by the day or for 5 hours, and some exotics also have a 3-hour rate. A few examples from the Las Vegas listings:

Booking more than a day? The second day is 50% off, or the third day is free. The Escalade has its own deal: rent 2 days and the 3rd is free. Supercar Experience also offers a price match guarantee.

03Book it

There are three steps:

  • Choose your car on the site and get in touch.
  • Send your details, insurance included, so everything is set up before you arrive.
  • Turn up at the agreed time. The car is ready when you get there.

You can text Supercar Experience at (725) 425-3583.

04The hand-over

This is the part Behind the Wheel films. In episode 3, a Huracán STO is delivered to a customer, with a check that everything's right. In episode 2, a Rolls-Royce Cullinan is delivered to a shoot, with a walk-through at the door.

05Not sure where to drive?

Supercar Experience runs guided drives from Las Vegas: Hoover Dam (4 hours, $1,099 per car), Red Rock Canyon (3 hours, $889 per car) and the Strip at night ($750 per car). To plan your own route, Supercar Experience's blog has a guide to scenic drives near Las Vegas.

06Before you book

  • 25 or older, or 21 to 24 and ready for the $299 fee
  • Your driver's license and insurance details to hand
  • Car picked, and the length: a day, 5 hours or 3 hours
  • A text to (725) 425-3583
