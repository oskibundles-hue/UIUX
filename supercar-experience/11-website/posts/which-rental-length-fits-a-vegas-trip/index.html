A day, 5 hours or 3 hours: which supercar rental fits your Vegas trip

The car is the easy part. The harder call is how long to keep it. Here's how to match a Supercar Experience rental length to the trip you've actually planned, and what each extra hour costs.

Supercar Experience · Las Vegas · rates from this site's fleet list, 7 Oct 2026

01Start with the trip, not the car

Before you look at prices, look at your Vegas schedule. Most trips fall into one of three shapes:

  • One free slot. A gap between a pool day and a show, or a morning before a flight. A short rental fits here: 5 hours, or 3 on the cars that offer it.
  • A day built around the car. A drive out of town, photos, dinner on the Strip after. That's a full day.
  • A weekend. If the car is the trip, book more than a day and use the multi-day deal: the second day is 50% off, or the third day is free.S1
What a "full day" means: 24 hours from pickup, or set pickup and return times? And are 5-hour and 3-hour rentals tied to set start times?

02What the extra hours cost

Across the 24 Las Vegas cars on this site with both rates, the 5-hour rate is 67% to 78% of the day rate.S1 Put the other way, moving up from 5 hours to a full day adds between $100 and $500. A spread of examples:

Ask how many miles come with the length you're booking before you plan a long drive out of town.

05When the day rate wins

Five hours already costs 67% to 78% of a full day, so the day rate is often the better buy when:

  • Your plan needs more than 5 hours with a little slack for traffic, photos and stops.
  • You want the car for both an afternoon drive and an evening out.
  • The car you want has no 5-hour rate.

The short rates win when the car fills one slot and the rest of the day is already booked.

06Pick your length

  • One planned drive, nothing else around it: 3 hours, where offered
  • An afternoon with room to wander: 5 hours
  • The car is the plan for the day: a full day
  • The car is the trip: 2 days or more, with the multi-day deal
