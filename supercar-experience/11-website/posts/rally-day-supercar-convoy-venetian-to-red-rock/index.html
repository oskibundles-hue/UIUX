Behind the Wheel · Episode 6

Rally day: a supercar convoy from the Venetian to Red Rock

On September 15, Supercar Experience took a group from Egnyte out for a rally day in Las Vegas. Episode 6 of Behind the Wheel films all of it, from the cars lined up in the garage to the last one back on the Strip. Here's how the day ran, stop by stop.S1

Supercar Experience · Las Vegas · filmed Sep 15, 2026 · full episode 2:55

Watch episode 6 · 2:55

01The lineup at the Venetian

The day starts in the garage at the Venetian, with every car for the convoy lined up before anyone arrives. The episode opens on it, and the lineup gets its own chapter 26 seconds in.S2

Which cars were in the lineup, and how many? Nothing in this site's files names them, so the post doesn't. A line like "a GT3 RS, an STO and …" would make this section much stronger.

02The safety talk, then everyone gets a car

When the Egnyte group arrives, nobody goes straight for the keys. First comes a safety talk for the whole group, and only then does everyone get a car. That's at 0:54 in the episode.S2

What does the safety talk cover? Even two or three of the actual rules (spacing, speed, what to do at lights) would help readers who are thinking of booking a group day.

03The run out: Blue Diamond to Red Rock

At 1:19 the convoy rolls out of the Venetian, past Blue Diamond and out to Red Rock. Red Rock is also where Supercar Experience's guided Red Rock Canyon drive goes: 3 hours, $889 per car.S2S3

Every Supercar Experience rental includes unlimited miles, so a run out to Red Rock and back doesn't add a mileage charge.S4

04Dinner at Red Rock

The convoy stops for dinner at Lotus of Siam in Red Rock Casino. That part of the episode starts at 1:28.S2

05The drive back down the Strip

At night the group heads back: the 215, 15 North, Flamingo and then the Strip, back to the Venetian. The rule for the drive back is simple: follow the car in front.S2
