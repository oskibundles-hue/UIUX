Renting a supercar in Las Vegas under 25: the 21 to 24 rule and the $299 fee

Turning 21 in Vegas, or here for a birthday or a bachelor party? You don't have to be 25 to drive a supercar on the Strip. Here's what Supercar Experience asks of drivers aged 21 to 24, and what to sort out before you book.

Supercar Experience · Las Vegas · terms checked 6 Oct 2026

01The rule

Supercar Experience's terms say the renter must be 25 or older. Drivers aged 21 to 24 can still rent, with a $299 underage fee on top of the rental.

The terms start at 21. There's no option listed for drivers younger than that.

02What you need, at any age

  • A valid driver's license.
  • Valid matching insurance. You send the details when you book, so they're checked before you arrive.

Matching insurance means full coverage of all three kinds:

CoverWhat it pays for
ComprehensiveTheft, vandalism, fire, weather and other damage that isn't from a collision
CollisionDamage when the car hits, or is hit by, another vehicle or object
LiabilityInjuries or damage you cause to other people

If you have full coverage on your own car in the US, in most cases it carries over to the rental.

03Check the gap

Some policies only pay up to the value of the car you own. Supercar Experience's own example: a policy that covers up to $60,000 leaves a $190,000 gap on a rental worth $250,000.

Excess Insurance Coverage closes that gap, for $249 to $499 depending on the car. Ask your insurer for your limit before you book.

No US insurance? Supercar Experience sells insurance for international drivers and anyone without a US policy.

05What it adds up to

Take a 5-hour rental of a Lamborghini Huracán EVO Spyder at $949. At 23, that's $949 plus the $299 fee: $1,248 before any extras. Rates for every car are on supercarexp.vip/cars, and our booking-to-keys guide walks through the rest of the rental.

Not sure which cars are open to you at your age? Text Supercar Experience at (725) 425-3583 before you book.

06Before you book

  • 21 or older, and ready for the $299 fee if you're under 25
  • Your driver's license
  • Full coverage insurance, and its limit checked against the car's value
  • A text to (725) 425-3583
