The eight rules

What makes an ad Locked-On

Each rule names the module that does the work in flash-special-showcase/ and the place it breaks, so nobody promises a shot the footage can't give.

1

Hook on frame 0

A black panel with the offer headline and the SE lockup, fully built on the first frame over a moving car shot. Stories use frame 0 as the preview, so nothing on it can still be animating in. An impact, 6 px shake and RGB split play under the first 8 frames only.

front.html · hook panel
Breaks when

The panel slides in. Re-hit it with a pulse and a glint instead of an entrance.

2

Badge lock-on

Gold corner brackets and a leader line are tracked onto the maker's badge in the footage, and the brand / model / year · class callout types on beside them. Lock-on ticks in the sound land on the snap.

lib/track.py · lib/lock.js
Breaks when

The car turns or foreshortens (the Black Series grille failed three ways). The box follows position and scale, not rotation. The shot needs to hold at least 1 s, so use the badge or a headlight.

3

Kinetic type and the slot reel

Type moves with true sub-frame motion blur (up to 10 samples per frame) and gold glints. The price spins as a slot reel and lands on the real figure: $1,200 in the reference.

lib/kinetic.js · lib/kcapture.js · lib/accum.py
Breaks when

Any reel frame shows a readable wrong digit. Check every landing frame at 100%; intermediates must be vertical smears.

4

Type behind the car

A giant word sits between the background and the car, with a slow 2.5D push. In the reference it's "RS" on the warehouse shot, because the hood is low on that side.

lib/matte_lib.py · lib/warehouse.py
Breaks when

The shot moves. It needs a locked-off shot and about 20 minutes of hand-tracing per car. A moving shot means frame-by-frame roto, so don't promise it. Watch for a dark seam trailing the car during the push.

5

Edit energy

Whips between shots, eased speed ramps (1.6× into 0.45× on the hero pass), light leaks, headlight streaks, a 5-frame black drop before the price, and one night grade across every shot.

lib/fx.py · lib/edl.py · lib/plate.py
Breaks when

Optical-flow slow-mo crosses a cut or runs longer than 0.7 s. Streak gain is too high and white wheels bloom. The same shot is used twice.

6

End card

Car name, offer lines, the price whole and clear of the car, then TEXT OR DM TO BOOK, the phone for the ad's city, the site, @SUPERCAR_EXPERIENCE_ and the requirements line. Up for at least 2.8 s.

front.html · end card
Breaks when

The roof or wing clips a digit, or the phone belongs to another city. brand.phone is the Scottsdale line.

7

Sound: the clip's own music

The source clip's music plays continuously and is never chopped at a cut. Designed accents (ticks, whooshes, impacts) sit about 45% under it. The music drops into its own tape stop, a beat of near-silence, then the end-card hit.

audio/bed_music.py
Breaks when

The clip is shorter than the ad. Extend it by whole bars on a downbeat, with no time-stretch over 3%. Don't layer a copy of the music on itself; hand over to it. Keep the last 50 ms silent.

8

Process

Every figure comes from a named source. Copy scores 5/5 in SlopMonster. Four reviewers (claims, brand, legibility, craft) check the render, and it gets fixed until nothing is blocking. Deliver a two-pass 11.5 Mb/s copy; the master stays in the repo.

build.py · deslop.py
Breaks when

A price, spec or promo goes on screen without a source. That's a hold, not a guess.

Choosing the treatment

Locked-On or Quick-Promo

Locked-On

The default for every SE car ad.

  • A few hours of build and four-lens review
  • Needs a badge shot of 1 s or longer; a locked-off shot for type behind the car
  • Reference: GT3 RS "LOCKED ON", 18 s
Quick-Promo

When a promo has to go out in under about 30 minutes.

  • Six fixed beats, 15 s, one clip
  • Brand panel, offer card, requirements card, end card
  • Reference: the 26 Sept flash-special quick cuts
